The voice switches on before the copying ever starts

A LoRA adapter on Qwen2.5-3B learns G.K. Chesterton's form — and the style–content frontier the research brief asked for turns out to be L-shaped: attribution jumps from 0.004 to 0.718 between 10k and 25k training words while verbatim leakage stays at exactly zero on every arm. Few-shot prompting shows the mirror image: no style, and wholesale copying. On-policy DPO then pushes the frontier higher still — to 0.845 — without touching either leakage or fluency, even when checked against its own preference targets.

Protocol: 7 nested corpus arms (one seeded shuffle), identical recipe, 252 blind-set generations per run, scored by one frozen verifier (held-out AUC 0.896 against 25 era-matched essayists). Base-model contamination ratio 0.90 — mild familiarity, discounted via floor runs. Seed cells: complete at 2k, two of three seeds at full. Assembled from 20+ machine-written run records.

1 · Style is a phase transition, not a slope

Between ~10 and ~55 gradient steps the voice snaps in. The dotted line — the verifier's mean continuous score — shows the same cliff, so this isn't a threshold artifact. Few-shot prompting stays at zero at every corpus size.

2 · Who actually leaks: the prompt, not the adapter

Few-shot prompting parrots its exemplars — up to 11.9% of generations contain a verbatim 12-gram of training text, and every copied run hits the 80-token measurement cap (whole passages, not phrases). The adapters, trained on the same text, copy nothing. Four of seven baselines fail the leakage gate outright.

4 · Where the voice lives: attention

The locus ablations (all at the full 179k-word corpus) reverse the recipe's assumption, and with the low-rank-MLP arm in, the pattern is monotone: the less MLP, the better — attention-only (0.857) > both-with-MLP-r8 (0.810) > MLP-only (0.794) ≈ both (0.790). Attention-only also uses 7.5M parameters against 31M, a third of the training time, and its fluency improved. Rank allocation is less a privacy dial than an efficiency dial: the MLP buys nothing here but fluency tax.

5 · Stage B sharpens the voice without stealing the words

On-policy DPO — the model's own drafts as rejected, the author's real passages as chosen — moves the frontier at both corpus sizes: +0.055 at full (0.790 → 0.845, the best number in the study) and 0.004 → 0.167 at 10k, conjuring measurable style where SFT alone had none. The suspicious reading — that pushing probability toward real passages teaches copying — was tested directly: Stage-B generations contain zero verbatim 12-grams of the very passages DPO optimized toward, and its stylometric distance (0.055) and fluency are both better than Stage A's. The KL anchor to Stage A plus one low-LR epoch sharpens the distribution without collapsing onto the targets.

Read with these caveats

  • Noise floor, partially measured. At 2k, adapter AV varies by ±0.004 across 3 seeds; at full, two of three seeds are in (0.790, 0.778 — range 0.012; seed 43 still running). Against that ~0.01 spread, attention-only's +0.067 margin and Stage B's +0.055 gain look real but stay provisional until the third seed lands.
  • The strict "crossover at 10k" is noise. Δ = +0.004 is one generation in 252. The real transition is the 10k → 25k cliff; the exact click-in point is what the per-checkpoint trajectory run will localize.
  • Entity emission is background, not leak. Adapters emit 1.8–3.3 author-corpus capitalized words per generation — but the bare base model emits 2.0 on the same prompts, top items are period-generic ("Church", "England"), and the counter has a known artifact (it counts "The"). Rates fall as corpus grows.
  • Scrubbing placeholders: trained adapters emit <PERSON>-style tags at roughly corpus rate — by design (the privacy mechanism made visible). Tested: tags depress the verifier, so attribution is earned despite them. Strip at serving time.
  • Still running: the last full-arm seed, the training-time trajectory (localizing the 10k→25k click-in), and the a14 no-scrub diagnostic that tests whether the recipe absorbs entities when scrubbing is off.
